Load-flow lab credits

Adapted from Advanced Power System Simulation & Scripting Lab by Mohd Aarish Shaheen, University of Kashmir.

This edition corrects the PV bus reactive injection sign, includes line charging in reported terminal flows and updates the pandapower export to use total line capacitance and retain PQ generation and bus shunts. It also reports missing slack buses, disconnected networks, invalid lines and convergence failure. The teaching model holds PV voltage setpoints without generator reactive limits.
