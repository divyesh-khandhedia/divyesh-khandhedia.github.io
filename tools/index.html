Grid equivalent from SCR

Convert a target short-circuit ratio and X/R into a positive-sequence Thevenin equivalent at the point of interconnection.

Method and assumptions

Uses a balanced three-phase fault, nominal prefault voltage, and grid short-circuit contribution excluding the plant under study. Sₛ꜀ = SCR × plant MW. |Z| = kV² / Sₛ꜀. Zbase = kV² / base MVA. SCR is a screening metric. Nearby inverter controls and site-specific criteria require separate assessment.

NERC · Short-Circuit Modeling and System Strength

Modal damping from eigenvalues

Paste continuous-time eigenvalues to compare frequency, damping ratio and growth or decay.

Paste CSV or tab-separated pairs. The optional first row can be real,imag.

Method and assumptions

For λ = σ + jω, f = |ω| / (2π) and ζ = −σ / |λ|. Envelope time constant = 1 / |σ|. The damping threshold applies to decaying oscillatory modes. Real modes use the sign of σ and have no oscillation frequency beyond 0 Hz. Each supplied row is reported, including conjugate pairs.

BESS ramp buffer

Estimate battery power and usable energy to hold grid import or generator output at a fixed target through a load profile.

Paste CSV or tab-separated pairs in time order. The optional first row can be time_s,load_mw.

Method and assumptions

Battery AC power is load minus target. Power varies linearly between samples. Stored energy falls at P/ηdis during discharge and rises at |P| × ηchg during charge. The minimum usable energy is the highest minus lowest stored-energy deviation, including crossings of the supply target. The estimate covers the supplied interval. For repeatable operation, restore any net energy change between events. Nameplate sizing also depends on the allowed SOC window, degradation and reserves.
